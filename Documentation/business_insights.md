# Business Insights — Olist E-Commerce Analytics

## 1. Project Objective

Analyze e-commerce sales, customers, product categories, payments, and delivery performance to identify patterns that can support business decisions.

## 2. Sales Performance

The initial SQL analysis calculated total item sales of approximately 13.59 million and freight charges of approximately 2.25 million in the dataset's currency.

**Business question:** How do sales and shipping charges change over time?

**Next step:** Analyze monthly sales trends to identify growth patterns and seasonal changes.

## 3. Product Category Performance

Initial analysis identified the following leading categories by item sales value:

* Health & Beauty
* Watches & Gifts
* Bed, Bath & Table
* Sports & Leisure
* Computers & Accessories

**Business question:** Which categories contribute most to sales?

**Next step:** Compare category sales, order volume, and average order value before recommending inventory or marketing priorities.

## 4. Delivery Performance

The dataset contains missing customer delivery dates for some orders.

**Business question:** Are delivery delays associated with lower customer review scores?

**Next step:** Compare delivery performance with review scores, while separating missing delivery dates from genuine delivery delays.

## 5. Customer Behavior

The dataset contains customer records and customer identifiers that can be used to investigate repeat purchasing.

**Business question:** How many customers place multiple orders, and how does their spending differ?

**Next step:** Analyze repeat purchase rates and customer spending using the appropriate customer identifier.

## 6. Conclusion

The initial analysis establishes a foundation for understanding sales, category performance, customer behavior, and delivery operations.

Further validated analysis is needed before making specific business recommendations.

## Project Status

Initial SQL analysis completed. Python analysis, dashboard validation, and additional business insights will be incorporated as the project develops.
